## Discipline

- **http_400_422_split**:
```text
invalid_argument(400) = 报文畸形不可解析；客户端重试无用
unprocessable_entity(422) = 语法可解析但语义校验失败；客户端改字段可成功

```
- **naming_forbidden**:
  - internal_error
  - revision_conflict
  - bad_request
  - invalid_dpop_proof_in_application_section
